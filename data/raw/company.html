Company Overview

About ABC Tech Solutions

ABC Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is an information technology services and product engineering company founded in 2014. The company designs, builds and maintains software for clients in banking, healthcare, retail and logistics. The headquarters is in Bengaluru, India, and the company has additional offices in Hyderabad and Chennai. ABC Tech currently has approximately 850 employees.

Services

ABC Tech Solutions offers the following services to its clients:

  • Custom software development and product engineering
  • Cloud migration and DevOps services
  • Data engineering, analytics and artificial intelligence solutions
  • Software testing and quality assurance
  • Cybersecurity consulting
  • Application maintenance and IT support

Mission and Vision

Mission: To help organisations build reliable, secure and useful technology that makes their work simpler.

Vision: To be the most trusted engineering partner for mid-sized enterprises in India by 2030.

Core Values

  1. Integrity - we are honest with clients, colleagues and ourselves.
  2. Customer Focus - we measure success by the outcomes our clients achieve.
  3. Innovation - we encourage curiosity and practical experimentation.
  4. Collaboration - we share knowledge and succeed as one team.
  5. Ownership - we take responsibility for our work from start to finish.

Job Roles and Career Levels

Technical roles at ABC Tech follow a six-level career framework. Each level has a typical experience range. Promotion depends on performance, demonstrated skills and business need, not on years of experience alone.

Career Levels

LevelTypical TitleTypical Experience
L1Associate Engineer0 to 2 years
L2Software Engineer2 to 4 years
L3Senior Software Engineer4 to 7 years
L4Lead Engineer7 to 10 years
L5Engineering Manager or Principal Engineer10 to 14 years
L6Director14 years or more

Role Responsibilities

  • Associate Engineer and Software Engineer: build and test features under guidance.
    • Write clean, tested and documented code.
    • Participate in code reviews and daily team meetings.
  • Senior Software Engineer and Lead Engineer: own modules and mentor others.
    • Design technical solutions and review the work of junior engineers.
    • Act as the technical contact for the client on assigned modules.
  • Engineering Manager and Principal Engineer: own delivery and technical direction for a team or a product area.
  • Director: responsible for a business unit, its people and its client outcomes.

Internal Transfers

Employees may apply for an internal transfer to another department after completing 12 months in their current role. The employee must inform their current manager before applying, and the transfer requires approval from both department heads and the HR team.

Working Hours and Attendance

Standard Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. This includes a one-hour lunch break from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM, so the standard working day is 8 hours of work. Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs.

Core Hours and Flexible Start

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. All employees are expected to be available for meetings during these hours. With the approval of their manager, an employee may start work at any time between 8:30 AM and 10:00 AM, as long as the full 9 hours at work, including the lunch break, are completed.

Attendance Recording

  • Office attendance is recorded using the ID card reader at the office entrance.
  • Remote work days are recorded by logging in to the ABC People portal before 10:00 AM.
  • Three late arrivals in a calendar month, without prior approval, are counted as a half-day of casual leave.

Overtime and Weekend Work

ABC Tech does not pay cash overtime. If an employee works on a weekend or a public holiday with prior written approval from their manager, they receive one compensatory off day for each day worked. Compensatory off days must be used within 60 days.

Work Model

Hybrid Work Policy

ABC Tech follows a hybrid work model. Employees work from the office on three days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote work days by default. Employees in client-facing roles may be required to follow a different schedule decided by their project.

Work From Anywhere

Employees who have completed their probation may work from a location outside their office city for up to 20 days per calendar year. The request must be approved by the manager at least 10 working days in advance, and the employee must remain reachable during core hours.

Remote Work Support

  • A one-time home office allowance of ₹15,000 for employees who are on the hybrid model.
  • An internet reimbursement of ₹1,000 per month, claimed through the ABC People portal.
  • A company laptop and a monitor on request through the IT helpdesk.

Leave Policy

Annual Paid Leave

Employees receive 24 paid leave days per year. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Paid leave is earned at the rate of 2 days per month from the date of joining, and employees who join in the middle of the year receive a proportionate number of days.

Leave TypeDays Per YearRules
Casual Leave8 Can be taken in half-day units. Maximum 3 consecutive days. Apply at least 1 day in advance except in emergencies.
Sick Leave8 A medical certificate is required if the employee is absent for more than 2 consecutive days.
Earned Leave8 For planned leave. Apply at least 2 weeks in advance for 5 or more consecutive days. Can be carried forward.
Total24Casual, sick and earned leave combined.

Carry Forward and Encashment

  • Only Earned Leave can be carried forward to the next leave year, up to a maximum of 8 days.
  • Unused Casual Leave and Sick Leave lapse on 31 March.
  • At the time of leaving the company, the carried-forward Earned Leave balance, up to a maximum of 16 days, is paid out at the employee's basic salary rate.

Public Holidays

ABC Tech provides 12 public holidays per year. The holiday list for each year is published by HR in January. In addition, every employee may choose 2 optional holidays from a list of 6 optional holidays.

Managers must approve or reject a leave request within 2 working days. If a leave request is not answered within 2 working days, HR sends a reminder to the manager.

Benefits

Health and Insurance

  • Group health insurance: cover of ₹5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. Parents can be added at an additional premium paid by the employee.
  • Term life insurance: cover equal to 3 times the employee's fixed annual CTC.
  • Personal accident insurance: cover of ₹10,00,000.
  • Annual health check-up: a free health check-up once a year for every employee.

Retirement Benefits

  • Provident Fund: the company and the employee each contribute 12% of the basic salary.
  • Gratuity: paid according to the Payment of Gratuity Act to employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service.

Learning and Development

  • Every employee has an annual learning budget of ₹40,000 for courses, books and conferences.
  • Approved professional certification fees are reimbursed in full after the employee passes the exam.
  • If an employee leaves within 12 months of receiving a certification reimbursement, they must repay the amount.

Wellness and Work-Life Support

  • An Employee Assistance Programme gives every employee 6 free confidential counselling sessions per year.
  • A gym and fitness reimbursement of up to ₹6,000 per year.
  • A subsidised cafeteria is available in the Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai offices.
  • A cab facility is provided for employees who work in the office beyond 8:00 PM.

Recognition

Managers can give spot awards for outstanding work. The company holds an annual awards event. Employees receive long service awards after 5 years and after 10 years of service.

Employee Referral Programme

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus after the new employee completes 6 months of service: ₹25,000 for hires at levels L1 to L3 and ₹50,000 for hires at level L4 and above.

Employee Policies

Code of Conduct

All employees must act honestly, treat colleagues and clients with respect, and follow the law. Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, age, disability, sexual orientation or nationality is not tolerated.

Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

ABC Tech has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. An Internal Committee investigates every complaint. A complaint should be submitted within 3 months of the incident, by email to posh@abctech.example. The identity of the person making the complaint is kept confidential.

Dress Code

The standard dress code is business casual. Casual clothing is allowed on Fridays. Formal business clothing is expected when meeting clients.

Confidentiality and Information Security

  • Every employee signs a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) on the day of joining.
  • Passwords must have at least 12 characters, and multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts.
  • Company laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are blocked.
  • A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT Support within 1 hour.
  • Confidential client code, client data and internal documents must not be pasted into public AI chat tools or shared on personal email or personal cloud storage.

Outside Work and Conflict of Interest

Employees may not take up paid work for another employer, or run a competing business, without written approval from HR. Employees must tell HR about any personal relationship or financial interest that could conflict with their duties.

Social Media

Employees may not share client names, project details or internal information on social media. Personal opinions must not be presented as the views of ABC Tech.

Travel and Expense Policy

HR Information

Onboarding

Every new employee attends an orientation session on the first day and is assigned a buddy for the first 90 days. The manager prepares a 30-60-90 day plan for the new employee.

New employees must submit the following documents on or before the joining date:

  • Government photo identity proof and address proof
  • Educational certificates
  • Relieving letter and last three payslips from the previous employer
  • Bank account details
  • Two passport-size photographs

Probation and Confirmation

The probation period is 3 months for levels L1 to L4 and 6 months for levels L5 and above. At the end of probation, the manager reviews the employee's performance, and HR issues a confirmation letter if the review is successful. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months.

Notice Period

Employee CategoryNotice Period
During probation30 days
Confirmed employees, levels L1 to L460 days
Confirmed employees, levels L5 and above90 days

A notice period buyout is possible only with the approval of the manager and HR. The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day.

Promotions

Promotion cycles take place twice a year, in April and in October. Employees are nominated by their manager and reviewed by a promotion committee.

Grievance Redressal

An employee with a workplace concern should follow these steps:

  1. Discuss the concern with their direct manager.
  2. If it is not resolved, raise it with their HR Business Partner.
  3. If it is still not resolved, escalate it to the Chief People Officer.

Internal Processes

How to Apply for Leave

  1. Log in to the ABC People portal.
  2. Open the Leave section and select Apply Leave.
  3. Choose the leave type, start date and end date, and write the reason.
  4. Submit the request. It goes to your manager for approval.
  5. You receive an email when the request is approved or rejected.

How to Claim an Expense Reimbursement

  1. Collect the original bills or receipts for the expense.
  2. Submit the claim in the ABC People portal within 30 days of the expense, with scanned receipts attached.
  3. Your manager approves the claim.
  4. The Finance team verifies the claim.
  5. The approved amount is paid within 10 working days with the next salary or as a separate payment.

Claims submitted after 30 days are not accepted unless the Finance Controller grants an exception.

How to Refer a Candidate

  1. Open the Referral section of the ABC People portal.
  2. Upload the candidate's resume and select the open position.
  3. HR contacts the candidate and updates the referral status in the portal.
  4. The referral bonus is paid after the new employee completes 6 months of service.

Company Rules

  • Employees must wear their ID card visibly while inside the office premises.
  • Visitors must register at reception and must be accompanied by an employee at all times.
  • Smoking and the use of alcohol or illegal substances are prohibited on company premises.
  • Company assets, such as laptops and phones, are to be used mainly for work and must be returned when the employee leaves.
  • Employees must follow fire safety instructions and take part in the annual fire drill.
  • Photography and video recording are not allowed in client work areas.
  • Breaking company rules may lead to disciplinary action, from a written warning up to termination of employment, depending on the seriousness of the violation.
